# Merchant Interview — template

**Register D.** One note per merchant interviewed. Results roll up into the pilot baseline metrics (commercial material, kept outside this repository) and
scoring into the merchant selection criteria (commercial material, kept outside this repository) .

## Interview rules

1. **Ask what they do, not what they want.** "Show me your last five sales" beats "would you use this?"
2. **Get numbers, not adjectives.** "Busy" is not data; "about 40 airtime sales a day" is.
3. **Never pitch during the baseline.** A merchant told how good Telga will be gives inflated answers.
4. **Never quote a commission or price.** Rates are NOT YET CONFIRMED and a quoted number becomes a promise.
5. **Get consent** before recording anything, and record **no customer data**.
